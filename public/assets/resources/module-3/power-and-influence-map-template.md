# Power and Influence Map Template

Use this template to identify who can enable change, who may block or delay it, whose voice should be strengthened, and what should be explored next. Keep entries safe and generalized. Do not include real names, exact sensitive locations, complaint details, survivor stories, accusations, confidential political details, or identifiable personal information.

## 3. Quadrant Logic

| Influence | Support / interest | Engagement meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High influence | High support / high interest | Work closely. These actors can help move change. |
| High influence | Uncertain or low support | Engage carefully. These actors can block, delay, filter, or weaken change. |
| Low or medium influence | High support / high interest | Strengthen voice safely. Rights-holder groups or allies may care deeply but lack power. |
| Low or medium influence | Low support / low interest | Monitor lightly. Keep aware, but do not spend most effort here unless their role changes. |

## 4. Design Implications

- Who needs early engagement?
- Whose voice should be strengthened safely?
- Who may filter information, block response, or delay change?
- What should the project design change before activities are finalized?

## 5. Carry Forward To Root Causes And Capacity Gaps

- Why might some rights-holder groups have low influence?
- Why might some responsible actors have limited support or motivation?
- What capacity, coordination, information, trust, accessibility, or accountability gaps should be explored next?
