# Asset Inventory and Substitution Rules

## Core rule

Use exact assets where available. If exact assets are unavailable, use screenshot-derived crops or neutral labeled placeholders. Do not use random stock images, AI-generated replacement photos, fake logos, or unrelated icons.

## Categories

### Category A — Must be exact or approved placeholder
UNICEF logo, UNICEF emblem, EU flag, European Commission/EU logo, Agora logo, player branding, toolbar icons, nav arrows, lock icons, completion icons, correct/incorrect icons, info/download icons.

Allowed fallback: labeled placeholder such as **UNICEF logo placeholder** or **EU flag placeholder**. Do not fake or redesign logos.

### Category B — Use screenshot-derived crop or structured placeholder
Course thumbnails, recommended course card images, module tile images, slide photographs, cotton field image, case characters, video posters, skyline illustration, mountain/path illustration, GMI diagram, timeline nodes, sticky notes.

Allowed fallback: labeled placeholder preserving size/aspect ratio, such as **Image placeholder: cotton field photo**.

### Category C — Pending assets
Missing slide images, missing resources, missing modal illustrations, missing quiz review visuals. Use **Pending source asset** or **Pending source content**.

## Placeholder design

Image placeholder: light grey/pale blue rectangle, border, centered label, correct aspect ratio.

Logo placeholder: simple bordered rectangle with label.

Pending asset: **Pending source asset**.

## Forbidden

Do not use random stock photos, unrelated icons, fake logos, recolored logos, cartoon replacements for photo-based screens, or invented resource pages.
