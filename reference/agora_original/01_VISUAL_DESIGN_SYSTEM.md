# Visual Design System

## Overall visual character

The product must feel like a formal UNICEF/Agora institutional e-learning platform: structured, content-first, humanitarian-sector oriented, formal but learner-friendly, and slide-based inside the course player.

## Core colors

### Outer Agora platform
- UNICEF/Agora turquoise blue: `#00A6C8` or `#009FBD` for top strip and section headers.
- Agora orange: `#F58220` or `#F28C28` for primary platform buttons.
- Completion green: `#4CAF50` or `#43A047`.
- Warning amber: `#F9A825` or `#F59E0B`.
- Error red: `#D93025` or `#DC2626`.
- Link blue: `#0073C8` or `#006FBF`.
- Page light grey: `#F3F4F6` or `#F5F5F5`.
- White: `#FFFFFF`.
- Body text: `#333333`.
- Borders: `#D9D9D9`, `#CCCCCC`, or `#E5E7EB`.

### Internal course player
- Header dark blue: `#005A78`, `#005F7F`, or `#00617F`.
- Dark teal slide panel: `#075B73` or `#0A5F77`.
- Yellow progress strip: `#D9A629` or `#E0B02E`.
- Slide heading dark blue: `#005A78` or `#005F7F`.
- Light blue info panels: `#E6F4FA` or `#EAF7FB`.
- Pale peach panels: `#F6DCCF` or `#F7DED2`.
- Pale green sticky notes: `#DDEEC6` or `#E0F0C9`.
- Quiz pale blue background: `#EAF6FB` or `#E7F5FA`.

## Typography

Use a clean sans-serif stack: `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` or `Roboto, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.

Outer platform section headers: white, bold, 18–22px. Course title: dark grey, bold, 28–36px. Body text: dark grey, 13–16px.

Internal player main slide titles: dark blue, bold, 34–44px. Start screen title: white, 44–58px. Slide body text: dark grey, 18–24px. Quiz answer text: 18–22px.

## Layout systems

### Outer platform
Use UNICEF strip → Agora header → breadcrumbs → page title → main layout. Course pages often use three columns: left cards, center content/module grid, right table of contents.

### Internal player
Use fixed full-screen shell: progress strip at very top, dark blue header, fixed left toolbar below header, main slide area to the right.

## Key component styles

- Platform buttons: orange with white text.
- Continue buttons: green with white text.
- Slide action buttons: dark blue pill with white text.
- Start/Exit buttons: white pill with blue text/icon.
- Modals: centered white rounded panel over dark overlay.
- Feedback modals: green/red header, white body, white circle icon, Continue button.
- Active tabs: dark blue with white text; inactive tabs: white with blue text.
- Radio selected state: filled blue dot; solution state uses green check and red X icons.

## Anti-modernization rules

Do not use glassmorphism, unrelated gradients, new color palettes, playful cartoon styling, extra animations, collapsed toolbars, side-panel modals, scrolling course pages, or generic LMS layouts.

Screen-specific descriptions override general visual rules.
