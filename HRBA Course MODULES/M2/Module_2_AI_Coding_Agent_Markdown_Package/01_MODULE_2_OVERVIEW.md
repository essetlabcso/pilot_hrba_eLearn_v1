# Module 2 Overview

## Course

**Applying the Human Rights-Based Approach (HRBA) in CSO Practice**

## Module title

**Module 2: Human Rights in Everyday CSO Work**

## Module role

Module 2 is the foundational concept-to-practice module. It helps participants move from seeing human rights as distant legal language to recognizing rights as practical standards that help CSOs understand dignity, exclusion, participation, responsibility, and accountability in everyday work.

## Recommended duration

**75–90 minutes**

## Primary audience

Local and grassroots CSO staff, leaders, volunteers, project teams, MEAL/accountability staff, advocacy/community engagement staff, and CSO focal persons.

## Main learner shift

From:

> “Human rights are distant legal language.”

To:

> “Human rights are practical standards that help CSOs understand dignity, exclusion, participation, responsibility, and accountability in everyday work.”

## Module learning outcomes

By the end of Module 2, participants should be able to:

1. explain human rights in simple, practical language;
2. recognize rights dimensions in everyday CSO issues;
3. describe key characteristics of human rights in plain language;
4. distinguish broad types of rights using practical examples;
5. use human rights standards safely as learning and analysis tools, not legal-advice shortcuts;
6. connect one CSO issue or project area to rights, exclusion, responsible actors, and improvement actions;
7. save a private portfolio checkpoint called **My Everyday Rights Lens**.

## Section sequence

1. **Start Module 2: Rights in Everyday CSO Work**
2. **What Are Human Rights?**
3. **Human Rights in Everyday Life**
4. **Characteristics of Human Rights**
5. **Types of Rights**
6. **Human Rights Systems and Standards**
7. **Connecting Human Rights to CSO Practice**

## Assessment rule

All Module 2 checks are formative only. They support practice and review. They do not issue a certificate, unlock a certificate, rank participants, or rank CSOs.

## Certificate rule

Certificate eligibility remains tied to the final course test with a score of **80% or above**.

## Main portfolio output

**My Everyday Rights Lens**

## Privacy rule

Portfolio inputs, optional notes, worksheet entries, and reflection outputs are private by default.

## Sensitive data rule

Do not ask learners to provide names, real cases, active complaints, safeguarding details, beneficiary lists, legal disputes, confidential documents, political details, raw organizational data, or uploads.
