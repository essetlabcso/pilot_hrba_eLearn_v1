# Module 1 Asset Placeholders and Alt Text

## Asset rule

Do not invent final asset filenames or paths. Use placeholders until approved filenames are provided.

## Visual style

Use DEC/CSO Learning Hub visual identity where relevant:

- primary blue: `#3B99D4`
- accent green: `#91C852`
- deep navy: `#0F172A`
- light background: `#F9FAFB`
- white cards
- dark text: `#111827`
- subtle borders: `#E5E7EB`
- rounded corners
- soft shadows

Visuals should feel locally grounded for Ethiopian or East African local/grassroots CSO practice.

Every image, diagram, roadmap, icon card, and visual comparison must have a text alternative. Do not rely on visual content alone to communicate required learning.
