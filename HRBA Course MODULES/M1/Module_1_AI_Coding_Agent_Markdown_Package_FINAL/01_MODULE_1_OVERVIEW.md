# Module 1 Overview

## Course

**Applying the Human Rights-Based Approach (HRBA) in CSO Practice**

## Module title

**Module 1: Starting Your HRBA Learning Journey — From Good Intentions to Rights-Based Practice**

## Module role

Module 1 is the course opening and foundation module. It welcomes participants, explains how the course works, establishes safe learning expectations, introduces HRBA as a practical approach for CSO work, and helps participants identify their own HRBA starting point through a private self-assessment and portfolio checkpoint.

## Target participants

Local and grassroots CSO staff, leaders, volunteers, project teams, MEAL staff, advocacy/community engagement staff, accountability focal persons, and CSO focal persons working in Ethiopian or East African civil society contexts.

## Module learning outcomes

By the end of Module 1, participants should be able to:

1. describe the purpose of the HRBA course and how to use the learning platform;
2. explain why HRBA is relevant to local and grassroots CSOs;
3. identify the basic shift from “beneficiaries” to “rights-holders”;
4. distinguish charity, needs-based, service-delivery, and rights-based practice;
5. identify rights-holders, duty-bearers, the CSO role, and other stakeholders in a simple fictional example;
6. complete a private HRBA starting-point self-assessment;
7. choose two HRBA priority areas for continued learning;
8. save one private Module 1 HRBA Learning Portfolio checkpoint.

## Module section flow

1. **Welcome and Safe Start**
2. **Why HRBA Matters for Local CSOs**
3. **The First HRBA Shift**
4. **Charity, Needs, Service Delivery, and Rights-Based Practice**
5. **Rights-Holders, Duty-Bearers, and the Role of CSOs**
6. **HRBA Starting Point Self-Assessment**
7. **Portfolio Checkpoint, Module Quiz, and Next Step**

## Assessment rule

All Module 1 checks are formative only. They support practice and review. They do not issue a certificate, unlock a certificate, rank participants, or rank CSOs.

## Certificate rule

Certificate eligibility remains tied to the final course test with a score of **80% or above**.

## Privacy rule

Self-assessment results, portfolio inputs, optional notes, and reflection outputs are private by default.

## Sensitive data rule

Do not ask learners to provide names, real cases, complaint records, safeguarding details, beneficiary lists, legal disputes, confidential documents, political details, or raw organizational data.
