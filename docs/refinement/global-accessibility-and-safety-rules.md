# Global Accessibility and Safety Rules V1

Date: 2026-06-08

## Accessibility

All screens must support:
- keyboard navigation;
- logical focus order;
- screen-reader labels;
- alt text for meaningful images and SVGs;
- text alternatives for diagrams, charts, hotspots, and connected maps;
- captions/transcripts for video or audio;
- readable color contrast;
- large tap targets;
- reduced-motion preference;
- mobile usability.

## Safety and privacy

Learners must not be asked to enter:
- real names of people or communities;
- active complaints;
- safeguarding or protection case details;
- legal disputes;
- confidential organizational records;
- beneficiary lists;
- raw organizational data;
- politically sensitive information.

Use safe wording such as:
“Keep your example broad and safe. Do not include names, active complaints, safeguarding details, confidential records, beneficiary lists, or sensitive organizational information.”

## Scenario rule

All examples and scenarios must be fictional, generalized, and non-identifying. Avoid wording that encourages learners to prove a rights issue by exposing real people.

## Assessment rule

Wrong answers should be plausible and reflect common weak practice. Avoid obviously silly answers.
