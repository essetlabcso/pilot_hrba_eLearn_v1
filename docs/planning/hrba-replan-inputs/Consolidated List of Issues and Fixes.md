G1 HRBA Content
Consolidated from the G1 Deep and Quick HRBA Content Review reports. The G1 reviews judge the course as ready with P1/P2
fixes before wider learner testing, with no clear P0 blocking issue, but with recurring fixes needed on duty-bearer logic, safe
advocacy, safeguarding, portfolio safety, local relevance, alt text, and ethical MEAL. The G1 checklist frames the review around
HRBA accuracy, local CSO relevance, safeguarding, ethics, inclusion, and rights-based representation.

interaction, realistic scenarios, feedback, assessment, portfolio outputs, and movement from “knowing” to “doing.”
